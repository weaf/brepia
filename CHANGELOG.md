# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- First public Brepia distribution generated from the qualified internal
  v1.8.0 engineering source.
- Public packaging is deterministic and records the exact engineering source,
  qualification tag and exporter revision in `brepia-release-provenance.json`.
- The supported product entry point is Brepia at `/`; obsolete product routes
  and engineering-only material are not part of this distribution.
